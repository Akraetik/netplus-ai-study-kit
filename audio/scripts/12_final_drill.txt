A closing thought on how CompTIA writes these questions.

The scenario will be longer than it needs to be. Read the last sentence first, because that is the actual question, then go back for the detail that discriminates.

Watch the qualifiers. Best, most likely, first. Multiple answers will work technically. The qualifier picks the winner.

Usually two answers are obviously wrong and two are plausible. The discriminator is almost always one word in the scenario. Multi vendor means open standard. Encrypted rules out the plain text options. Without changing every device means centralized. During an outage means out of band.

And insecure protocols are almost never the right answer in a security question. Telnet, FTP, PPTP, WEP, SNMP version one and two c, WPS, SMB version one. Those exist in the answer list to be eliminated.
