# 3. Pick a Plan

Your timeline sets your plan. Be honest about hours. A plan you can't keep is worse than a slower plan you can.

| Plan | Total time | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [7-day sprint](#7-day-sprint) | ~55–60 hrs | Some networking background, a week off, a hard deadline |
| [30-day standard](#30-day-standard) | ~60–75 hrs | Most people with a job |
| [60-day part-time](#60-day-part-time) | ~70–90 hrs | New to networking, or limited evenings |

All three plans share the same skeleton:

1. **Baseline** — one cold practice test.
2. **Coverage** — see every objective once (video course or book).
3. **Consolidation** — cards and audio daily; practice questions by domain.
4. **Adaptive loops** — full practice tests → miss log → rebuilt material. Repeat.
5. **Readiness gate** — three different exams ≥ 85%, no domain < 75%. Then book.

---

## 7-day sprint

This is the plan I used. It works only if you already have some exposure and can give it about 8 hours a day.

| Day | Morning (3 hrs) | Afternoon (3 hrs) | Evening + commute (2 hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold practice test + miss log | Domain 1: OSI, ports, protocols | Import Rapid Recall deck; audio tracks 01–04 |
| 2 | Domain 1: addressing, subnetting (by hand) | Domain 2: routing, switching, VLANs | Anki; audio 05–08 |
| 3 | Domain 2: wireless, physical | Domain 3: operations, monitoring, DR | Anki; audio 09–12 |
| 4 | Domain 4: security | Domain 5: methodology, tools | **Full practice test #2** → miss log → AI diagnosis |
| 5 | Rebuild: trouble-area cards + audio from misses | Targeted review of top 3 weak objectives | **Full practice test #3** → loop |
| 6 | Weak-objective drills | **Full practice test #4** → loop | Trouble-area deck only |
| 7 | **Full practice test #5** | Final loop on remaining misses | Light review. Sleep. |

Book the exam when the readiness gate is met, not before. On day 7 you may need a few more days. That's normal.

## 30-day standard

About 2–2.5 hours a day plus commute audio.

| Week | Focus | Practice tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline test. Domain 1 in full. Start Rapid Recall deck and ports audio. | Baseline + Domain 1 quiz |
| 2 | Domains 2 and 3. Continue daily Anki (keep new cards at 20–30/day). | Domain quizzes |
| 3 | Domains 4 and 5. Start full-deck interleaved reviews. | **Full test #1** on day 21 → loop |
| 4 | Adaptive loops only. Trouble-area deck. Miss-driven audio. | Full tests #2–#5, one every 1–2 days |

## 60-day part-time

About 1–1.5 hours on weekdays plus commute, 3 hours on one weekend day.

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Baseline. Domain 1. Subnetting practice every day for 15 minutes. |
| 3–4 | Domain 2. Build your own cards for anything the decks don't cover well. |
| 5 | Domain 3. |
| 6 | Domains 4 and 5. |
| 7 | Full test #1 and #2. Two adaptive loops. |
| 8 | Full tests #3–#5. Loops. Readiness gate. Book it. |

---

## Rules that apply to every plan

- **Anki every day.** Missing a day doubles tomorrow's load. Fifteen minutes on a bad day beats zero.
- **Cap new cards.** 20–40 new cards a day is plenty. Reviews grow about 5–10× your daily new count.
- **Subnet by hand.** Don't let audio or cards replace pencil work. Aim for 30 seconds per question by exam week.
- **Full tests under exam conditions.** 90 minutes, no notes, no pausing. Review misses the same day.
- **Log every miss.** See [the adaptive loop](06-adaptive-loop.md).
- **Protect sleep.** Memory consolidation happens during sleep. All-nighters undo the spacing you paid for.
