# 1. Define the Target

Vague goals produce vague studying. "Learn networking" has no finish line. "Pass N10-009 by October 30 with three practice scores of 85% or better first" does.

Write down four things before you open a single video.

## 1. The exam

**CompTIA Network+ N10-009.** Know its shape.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Questions | Up to 90. Multiple choice (single and multiple answer) and performance-based questions (PBQs) |
| Time | 90 minutes |
| Passing score | 720 on a scale of 100–900 |
| Recommended experience | CompTIA suggests A+ and 9–12 months of networking work. Not required. |
| Objectives | The official PDF from CompTIA. **This is your syllabus.** Download it. |

Confirm the numbers on [comptia.org](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/network) before you plan. CompTIA revises exams about every three years.

## 2. The domains and their weights

| Domain | Weight | What it really tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 Networking Concepts | 23% | OSI model, ports and protocols, addressing and subnetting, topologies, cloud concepts |
| 2.0 Network Implementation | 20% | Routing, switching and VLANs, wireless standards, physical installation |
| 3.0 Network Operations | 19% | Documentation, monitoring, SNMP and syslog, high availability, disaster recovery |
| 4.0 Network Security | 14% | Attacks, hardening, AAA, segmentation, physical security |
| 5.0 Network Troubleshooting | 24% | The CompTIA methodology, cable issues, wireless issues, the right tool for the symptom |

Weights tell you where the points are. Domains 1 and 5 together are nearly half the exam. Domain 5 questions often pull knowledge from all the others.

## 3. Your starting point

Take one full practice test **cold**, before you study. It will hurt. That's fine. The score is your baseline, and the misses are your first map.

Record it in [`templates/score-tracker.csv`](../templates/score-tracker.csv).

## 4. Your finish line

Define "ready" as a number, not a feeling. This kit uses:

> **Ready = three different practice exams at 85% or better, taken in the last 7 days, with no domain under 75%.**

Practice exams tend to run harder than the real thing, but a single good score can be memorization. Three different exams at 85% is a signal.
