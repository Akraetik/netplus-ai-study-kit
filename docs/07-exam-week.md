# 7. Exam Week

## Before you book

- Readiness gate met (see [the adaptive loop](06-adaptive-loop.md#step-7--check-the-readiness-gate)).
- Voucher bought. Students: check the CompTIA Academic Store for a discounted voucher. Veterans: check whether your education benefits or a transition program covers it.
- Choose **test center** or **online proctored** (Pearson OnVUE). Online needs a clean desk, a webcam, a stable connection, and a system check done in advance. A test center removes those risks.

## The last 72 hours

| When | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| 72 hrs out | One last full practice test. One last loop on its misses. | Start new resources. |
| 48 hrs out | Trouble-area deck. Confusion-killer audio. Ports drill. Subnetting: 20 quick problems. | Take three more full tests "just to be sure." |
| 24 hrs out | Light Anki reviews. One commute's worth of audio. Confirm time, location, ID. | Cram. Stay up late. |
| Exam morning | Ports drill in the car. A normal breakfast. Arrive early. | Learn anything new. |

## In the exam

- **Performance-based questions usually come first.** If one is eating your time, flag it, skip it, and come back. Multiple-choice questions are faster points.
- **Budget about one minute per question.** Check the clock at question 30 and 60.
- **Read the last sentence first.** Find the qualifier: BEST, FIRST, MOST likely, NOT.
- **Eliminate, then choose.** Two answers are usually clearly wrong. Decide between the other two.
- **For troubleshooting questions, walk the methodology.** Identify the problem → theory → test → plan → implement → verify → document. "What should the technician do FIRST?" is usually the earliest step not yet done.
- **Flag and move on.** Don't let one question cost you three.
- **Change an answer only for a reason.** A new fact you remembered is a reason. A vague feeling isn't.

## Afterward

**Passed?** Add it to LinkedIn and your résumé the same day. Upload the badge from Credly. Then write down what worked while you remember. Open an issue or PR here if the kit helped. I'd like to know.

**Didn't pass?** Your score report lists weak areas by objective. That's a free miss log. Run it through the [analysis prompt](../prompts/04-practice-test-analysis.md), run two or three loops, and rebook. Check CompTIA's current retake policy first.
